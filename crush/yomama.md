# 10 Yo Mama Jokes

1. Yo mama so old, her Social Security number is 1.
2. Yo mama so fat, when she wears a yellow raincoat, people yell "Taxi!"
3. Yo mama so dumb, she thought a quarterback was a refund.
4. Yo mama so clumsy, she got tangled up in a cordless phone.
5. Yo mama so dumb, she stared at a cup of orange juice for 12 hours because it said "concentrate."
6. Yo mama so old, she knew Burger King when he was still a prince.
7. Yo mama so dumb, she sold her car for gas money.
8. Yo mama so fat, when she sits around the house, she sits *around* the house.
9. Yo mama so dumb, she put two quarters in her ears and thought she was listening to Wu-Tang.
10. Yo mama so dumb, she thought Dunkin' Donuts was a basketball team.
